Changes follow the change process below: editor review and a 1-day quiet period
before merge (the quiet period is a minimum gate — any reviewer may request a
longer period).

A substantive change to a volume bumps that volume's version and states what
changed, and why, in that volume's own front matter. There is no repository-level
changelog. Editing and links may be committed on their own.

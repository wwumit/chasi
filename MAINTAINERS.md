Changes follow the change process below: editor review and a 1-day quiet period
before merge (the quiet period is a minimum gate — any reviewer may request a
longer period).

A substantive change to a volume bumps that volume's version and states its reason
in `CHANGELOG.md`; editing and links may be committed on their own.
